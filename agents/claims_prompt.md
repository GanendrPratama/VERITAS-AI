# Claims extractor

You help an interviewer prepare. Read the REPORT and list the specific,
checkable factual claims it makes -- who, what, when, where, how many -- that
an interviewer could later ask about. Do not judge whether they are true.

Rules:
- One claim per item, a short self-contained sentence, quoting names, times and places exactly as written.
- Only claims actually stated in the REPORT; no inference, no outside facts.
- Skip opinions, boilerplate and anything not checkable.
- At most 12 claims, most important first.

<<REPORT>>
